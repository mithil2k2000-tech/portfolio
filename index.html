About me

Engineer who ships the whole thing.

My path into software engineering started in data. Before moving to the UK, I spent four years as a Quality Analyst, reviewing datasets for international clients including Forbes and Australian universities. That is where I learned to care about what a pattern in the data actually means, not just that it is there.

Since relocating to London I have been completing my MSc in Cloud Computing at the University of East London, while working across cloud engineering, data advisory and digital operations. Along the way I have written the backend, the frontend, the tests and the infrastructure for every project on this page: a TypeScript payments platform, a Go payroll engine, Python APIs and tools, and C#/.NET serverless functions.

That shows up as a habit now: proper access control, observability, and quality gates, not a demo that only survives the happy path. I am just as comfortable explaining a design tradeoff to stakeholders as I am debugging a race condition in a payment flow. I build with AI coding tools every day, and keep the decisions that matter deterministic and tested.
